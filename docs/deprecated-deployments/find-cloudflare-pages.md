# How to Find Cloudflare Pages

## Method 1: Direct Link
Go directly to: **https://dash.cloudflare.com/pages**

## Method 2: Navigate in Dashboard

### Step 1: Login to Cloudflare
- Go to https://dash.cloudflare.com/
- Make sure you're logged into the correct account

### Step 2: Look for Pages
Cloudflare Pages might be located in different places depending on your account:

**Option A: Left Sidebar**
- Look for "Pages" in the left sidebar menu
- Sometimes it's under a "Products" or "Workers & Pages" section

**Option B: Top Navigation**
- Look for "Workers & Pages" in the top menu
- Click it, then look for a "Pages" tab

**Option C: Account Dashboard**
- From your main dashboard, scroll down
- Look for a "Pages" card/tile in the products grid

**Option D: Search**
- Use the search bar at the top of Cloudflare dashboard
- Type "pages" or "workers"

## Method 3: Alternative Access

### Via Workers Dashboard
1. Go to **Workers & Pages** (if you see this)
2. Click the **"Create"** button
3. Choose **"Pages"** tab

### Via Zero Trust (if visible)
- Sometimes Pages appears under Zero Trust products

## What if Pages isn't visible?

### Account Permissions
- Make sure you have admin/owner access to the Cloudflare account
- Some restricted accounts don't show all products

### Account Type
- Pages should be available on all account types (free included)
- If not visible, your account might have restrictions

### Fallback Options
If you can't find Pages, you have alternatives:

1. **Upload via FTP/SFTP** to your current web server
2. **Use Cloudflare Tunnel** with a local web server
3. **Deploy to Netlify/Vercel** then point DNS to them
